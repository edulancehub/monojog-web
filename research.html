01 — WHAT WE’RE TRYING TO LEARN

Better questions,
before bigger claims.

Monojog Shield is a hardware research concept. We’re working out what’s technically possible, what could be genuinely useful, and what should remain out of bounds.

01 / SIGNAL

What can we sense?

Explore whether brain-activity patterns can be captured consistently and comfortably in everyday settings—not just in ideal conditions.

02 / CONTEXT
+◌↗

What does a signal mean?

Understand how personal reflection, environment, rest, and task context matter when someone makes sense of a measurement.

03 / CARE
✳

How should insight feel?

Find out whether feedback can encourage curiosity and agency without creating pressure, surveillance, or false certainty.

02 — WHAT THIS IS / ISN’T

Research first.
No magic brain score.

Brain activity is complex and changes with many factors. A wearable signal alone cannot tell the whole story of someone’s attention, wellbeing, or brain health.

We are not offering a validated brain-fitness assessment, diagnosis, treatment, or medical device. We will be clear about uncertainty and limitations as the work develops.
