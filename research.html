01 — WHAT GUIDES US

Technology should
know its place.

Our north star is not more monitoring. It’s better understanding, shaped with the people it’s meant to serve.

01

People stay in control

Signals and prompts should be understandable, optional, and easy to dismiss. A tool should support your choices, never make them for you.

↗
02

Privacy is part of care

Brain data is personal. We aim to minimize collection, explain how information is used, and make privacy decisions visible.

↗
03

Evidence before claims

Brain activity is complex. We won’t present a signal as a diagnosis, a measure of a person’s worth, or a complete picture of brain health.

↗
04

Research should be shared

We want to make our questions, design choices, and learnings open to discussion so the work can improve through collaboration.

↗
02 — HONEST ABOUT THE LIMITS

A signal is not
the whole story.

Brain-sensing tools may one day support personal reflection, but a signal can be affected by context and many other factors. It cannot, on its own, define someone’s brain fitness or diagnose a health condition.

Monojog Shield is an early research concept. We’re exploring questions, not offering a clinically validated product or medical advice.
