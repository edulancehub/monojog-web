01 — THE QUESTION

What if brain signals could help you know yourself better?

Monojog Shield is our exploration of a small, wearable brain-sensing device. We’re asking whether patterns in brain activity—considered alongside a person’s own experience and context—could offer useful reflections about attention and mental routines.

We’re still researching what can be measured reliably, what information is genuinely helpful, and how to make the technology comfortable and understandable. We won’t claim a device can tell you how healthy your brain is from one reading.
