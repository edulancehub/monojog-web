01 — WHY WE’RE HERE A different kind of focus company

Focus isn’t a score to chase.
It’s part of being human.

Our attention shifts with our surroundings, energy, and needs. Instead of asking people to push harder, we want to create tools that help them notice what’s happening and choose what helps.

That means designing with care, being honest about what technology can and can’t tell us, and making room for the people who will use—and study—these tools.

03 — WHAT WE BELIEVE

Technology should
know its place.

Our north star is not more monitoring. It’s better support, shaped with the people it’s meant to serve.

01

People stay in control

Prompts should be understandable, optional, and easy to dismiss. A tool should support your choices, never make them for you.

↗
02

Privacy is part of care

We aim to minimize what is collected, explain what data is used, and make privacy decisions visible—not hidden in fine print.

↗
03

Evidence before claims

Attention is complex. We won’t present a signal as a diagnosis, or suggest a prototype can prevent accidents or replace professional advice.

↗
04

Research should be shared

We want to make our questions, design choices, and learnings open to discussion—so this work can improve through collaboration.

↗
04 — MONOJOG SHIELD / OPEN RESEARCH

Let’s explore
attention together.

Monojog Shield is an open research project, not a finished medical or safety product. We’re beginning with careful questions: how can technology support attention without adding pressure, invading privacy, or overstating what it knows?

We’ll share the direction as it develops and welcome thoughtful feedback from researchers, designers, and people with lived experience.
