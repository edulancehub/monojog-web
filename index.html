01 — OUR POINT OF VIEWBrain health, with people at the center

Your brain is more than
a number on a screen.

Brain activity is complex, personal, and always changing. We’re interested in hardware that can help people explore their own patterns—not reduce a person to a score or tell them how they should feel.

Our goal is to make brain-sensing technology more understandable and supportive, with privacy, choice, and scientific care built in from the beginning.
